Brewprint

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

The short version

  • Brewprint keeps the coffees, tastings and photos you log, so you can see them on any device you sign in to.
  • Your friends see only the tastings you choose to share with them.
  • There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, at any time.
  • Brewprint is for people 18 and over.

Who we are

Brewprint is a coffee-tasting journal made by Vico Wong, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Write to vico.wch@gmail.com with any question about your data or this policy.

Your friends see your name and the tastings you share with them, including the photo, recipe and notes. They don't see anything you haven't shared, and nobody else can see your tastings.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. We'd only disclose it to anyone else if the law required us to.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything you've logged: until you delete them, or your account.
  • Usage records for daily limits: 30 days.
  • Technical logs: a short time, under Supabase's own retention.
  • Reports: until they're reviewed and dealt with, and in any case they're deleted when either account involved is deleted.
  • When you delete something, it's removed straight away, though it may stay in Supabase's backups for a short time before they expire.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy to take elsewhere, restrict how we use it, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app: you can edit or delete anything you've logged, stop sharing a tasting, and delete your whole account (see Delete your data). For anything else, email vico.wch@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

Brewprint is only for people aged 18 or over, and everyone confirms their age before using it. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and Supabase. The database only lets each person reach their own data, plus what friends have chosen to share with them. Photos are kept in private storage and opened through short-lived links.

Automated decisions

Brewprint's statistics and suggestions, such as Coffee DNA, come only from your own ratings. They don't affect your rights or access to anything.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will ask you to read and agree to the new version before you carry on.
