How to delete your Brewprint account, or just some of your data

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Brewprint and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account and privacy, tap Delete account and all data.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't use the app, email vico.wch@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Brewprint account", from the Google email address you sign in with, so we can tell the account is yours. We'll delete it and confirm within one month, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Everything: your account, coffees, bags, tastings, notes, photos, friendships, invite code, blocks, reports you've made or that were made about your brews, feedback, and usage records. Friends stop seeing your shared brews immediately. Some data may stay in Supabase's backups for a short time until they expire. Nothing is kept beyond that.

Delete only some of your data

  • A tasting, coffee or bag: open it and delete it.
  • A photo: open the tasting and remove the photo.
  • Stop sharing a tasting: turn off "Share with friends" on it. Friends stop seeing it at once.
  • A friend: Profile, then Friends, then Remove or Block.

For anything else, email vico.wch@gmail.com.
