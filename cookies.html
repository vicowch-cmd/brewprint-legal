Brewprint

Cookie Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

The short version

Brewprint sets no cookies, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. In a web browser it keeps only what it needs to work, so there's nothing to accept or refuse.

What the web app stores in your browser

Brewprint uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, for:

WhatWhyHow long
Your sign-in sessionKeeps you signed in.Until you sign out.
A copy of your coffees and tastingsMakes the app quick, and lets it work offline.Until you sign out or clear your browser's data.
An unfinished tastingSo you don't lose it if the page reloads.Until you finish or start over.
That you've seen the storage noticeSo it isn't shown again.Until you clear your browser's data.

All of these are strictly necessary for the service you've asked for, so under the UK's Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations they don't need your consent. You can clear them at any time in your browser's settings, though you'll then be signed out.

Other sites

When you sign in, you're taken to Google's sign-in page, which uses Google's own cookies under Google's cookie policy. Links to roasters' websites follow those sites' own policies.

The phone apps

The iPhone and Android apps don't use cookies. They keep the same things as above in the app's own storage on your phone, and that's removed if you delete the app.
